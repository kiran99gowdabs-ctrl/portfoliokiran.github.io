Case Studies

Three examples of how I approach engineering problems.

Automation

Knowing What to Automate, and How

Challenge
Not every engineering task is worth automating, and the wrong choice wastes time and money.
What to Automate
I assess each task by time spent, error risk, cost saving, ROI and payback. Manual drawing comparison was slow and easy to get wrong, so it was a clear candidate.
How to Automate
I match the tool to the task: VBA for Excel-based processes and Python for drawing checks. I built a pixel-level comparison tool, then extended it to PDF sets, moved items, Adobe-edited and scanned drawings, with Excel reporting.
Result
Validation time reduced from hours to minutes, with adoption across multiple programmes.
